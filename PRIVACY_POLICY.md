# MobiLine Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 1, 2026_

MobiLine ("we", "us") provides this privacy policy to explain what information our mobile application collects.

## Data Collection
MobiLine does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app processes all measurements locally on your device.

## Sensor Data
MobiLine uses your device's sensors (accelerometer, gyroscope, rotation vector) solely to measure camber and toe. This data is processed in real time on your device and is not recorded, logged, or transmitted to us or to any third party.

## Internet Access
MobiLine does not access the internet. It makes no network requests and communicates with no external servers.

## Permissions
The app only requires access to your device's motion sensors, used exclusively for measurement.

## No Account, No Ads, No Analytics
MobiLine requires no account, contains no advertising, and integrates no third-party analytics or tracking software.

## Children's Privacy
MobiLine does not collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. We will post any changes here with an updated revision date.
